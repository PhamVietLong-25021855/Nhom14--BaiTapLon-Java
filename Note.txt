So sanh 2 project: Long vs Nguyen Duc Anh

1. Ket luan nhanh

- Hai ban rat gan nhau. Phan lon code giong nhau.
- Khac biet thuc su chi tap trung o 9 file:
  - data/auctions.txt
  - data/bids.txt
  - data/homepage-announcements.txt
  - data/users.txt
  - resources/userauth/gui/fxml/style.css
  - resources/userauth/gui/fxml/bidder-dashboard-view.fxml
  - src/userauth/dao/AuctionDAOImpl.java
  - src/userauth/dao/AutoBidDAOImpl.java
  - src/userauth/database/DatabaseInitializer.java
- Diem khac nhau quan trong nhat:
  - Ban Long co auto bid "thuc thi that" bang trigger/function trong PostgreSQL.
  - Ban Duc Anh chi co auto bid o muc luu cau hinh + UI + CRUD, chua co co che tu dong bid khi gia thay doi.

2. Khac nhau ve code

2.1. DatabaseInitializer.java

Ban Long:
- Tao bang auto_bids.
- Tao function handle_auto_bidding().
- Tao trigger trigger_auto_bid tren bang auctions.
- Sau khi current_highest_bid thay doi, DB se tiep tuc xet cac auto bid va tu dong chen them bid vao bang bids.

Ban Duc Anh:
- Chi tao bang auto_bids.
- Khong co function handle_auto_bidding().
- Khong co trigger trigger_auto_bid.
- Khong co synchronizeDatabaseObjects().

Y nghia:
- Long da di den muc "engine" cho auto bid.
- Duc Anh moi dung o tang luu du lieu, chua co tang thuc thi.

2.2. bidder-dashboard-view.fxml

Ban Long:
- Co man hinh quan ly auto bid.
- Nut "Change Password" nam rieng trong khu AUCTION LIST.
- Khong gan them style.css rieng cho TableView.
- Khu "LIVE BID FEED" dung truoc khu "Auto Bidding".

Ban Duc Anh:
- Them MenuItem "Change password" vao SETTINGS menu.
- Bo nut "CHANGE PASSWORD" rieng trong panel chinh.
- Gan stylesheets="@style.css" cho tableAuctions va tableAutoBid.
- Di chuyen panel "Auto Bidding (Current auction)" len tren panel "LIVE BID FEED".
- Them file style.css de chinh padding/cell/scrollbar cho table.

Y nghia:
- Duc Anh co chinh sua UI ro hon, dac biet o khu bidder dashboard.
- Long nghieng ve tinh nang, Duc Anh nghieng ve trinh bay va thao tac UI.

2.3. style.css

Chi co o ban Duc Anh:
- Chinh padding cho table.
- Chinh fixed-cell-size.
- Chinh can le table-cell.
- Giu scrollbar hien thi ro hon.

Y nghia:
- Day la phan UI polish.
- It anh huong business logic, co the nhap rieng de lam giao dien de nhin hon.

2.4. AuctionDAOImpl.java

Ban Long:
- Khi update auction loi, thong diep exception co them SQLState va detail.

Ban Duc Anh:
- Chi nem loi tong quat "Unable to update the auction in PostgreSQL."

Y nghia:
- Ban Long de debug tot hon.
- Ban Duc Anh gon hon nhung kho truy vet loi hon.

2.5. AutoBidDAOImpl.java

Ban Long:
- Con dong System.out.println(resultSet) trong findAllUserAutoBid().

Ban Duc Anh:
- Da bo dong debug print do.

Y nghia:
- Ban Duc Anh sach hon, tranh log rac.

2.6. Cac file data/*.txt

Chi co o ban Duc Anh:
- data/auctions.txt
- data/bids.txt
- data/homepage-announcements.txt
- data/users.txt

Y nghia:
- Day co ve la du lieu mau hoac dau vet tu co che luu file cu.
- Neu project dang chay theo PostgreSQL la chinh, cac file nay khong phai phan merge quan trong.

3. Khac nhau ve cach thuc thi

3.1. Ban Long

Cach thuc thi auto bid:
- User tao cau hinh auto bid.
- Cau hinh duoc luu vao bang auto_bids.
- Khi gia auction thay doi, trigger o DB duoc kich hoat.
- Function handle_auto_bidding() tu tim auto bid hop le, tinh next bid, chen bid moi, cap nhat winner va current_highest_bid.

Dac diem:
- Auto bid chay o tang database.
- Co tinh "tu dong thuc su".
- It phu thuoc vao UI.
- Manh hon cho logic dong thoi.

Rui ro:
- Kho debug hon vi logic nam trong PostgreSQL.
- Can quan ly schema/trigger can than.

3.2. Ban Duc Anh

Cach thuc thi auto bid:
- User tao/sua/xoa auto bid tren UI.
- Du lieu duoc luu vao bang auto_bids.
- Khi dat bid thuong, khong thay co co che nao tiep tuc kich auto bid cua nguoi khac.

Dac diem:
- De hieu hon o muc code Java va UI.
- Co giao dien quan ly auto bid gon hon.
- Nhung chua du de goi la auto bidding hoan chinh.

Rui ro:
- User nghi rang da co auto bid, nhung thuc te he thong chi luu cau hinh.
- Tinh nang de gay hieu nham neu dem vao ban final.
