BAO CAO TICH HOP CHUC NANG WALLET

1. Muc tieu
- Tich hop chuc nang wallet vao ban hien tai theo kien truc client-server.
- Wallet ho tro nap tien, xem so du, giu tien khi dang dan dau phien dau gia, tru tien khi chot thanh cong, va hoan/tra lai tien khi huy ket qua.

2. Hien trang truoc khi tich hop
- Ban hien tai da tach thanh 3 module: core-common, server, client.
- He thong da co luong dang ky, dang nhap, dau gia, auto-bid, homepage.
- Chua co model wallet, chua co bang wallet trong database, chua co network action de client goi wallet.
- AuctionService chi xu ly bid va settlement theo trang thai, chua rang buoc den so du tai khoan.

3. Noi dung da thuc hien

3.1. Bo sung lop dung chung trong core-common
- Them WalletApi.
- Them WalletController.
- Them cac model:
  + Wallet
  + TopUpTransaction
  + PaymentMethod
  + TopUpStatus
- Them cac action mang:
  + WALLET_GET
  + WALLET_TOP_UP
  + WALLET_TOP_UP_HISTORY

3.2. Bo sung backend trong server
- Them WalletDAO va WalletDAOImpl.
- Them WalletService de xu ly nghiep vu wallet.
- Cap nhat DatabaseInitializer de tao cac bang:
  + wallets
  + topup_transactions
- Cap nhat AuctionRequestHandler de xu ly request wallet tu client.
- Cap nhat ServerContext de khoi tao WalletService va WalletController.
- Cap nhat AuthService de wallet duoc tao/lazy-create khi user moi duoc dang ky.

3.3. Gan wallet vao nghiep vu dau gia
- AuctionService da duoc mo rong de:
  + kiem tra available balance truoc khi chap nhan bid
  + reserve tien cho bidder dang dan dau
  + release reserved funds cho bidder bi outbid
  + xu ly auto-bid co rang buoc so du
  + capture reserved funds khi seller mark auction as paid
  + refund hoac release funds khi ket qua auction bi huy
- Luong settlement duoc sua de trang thai PAID va CANCELED khong chi doi status ma con cap nhat wallet dung nghiep vu.

3.4. Bo sung client
- Them RemoteWalletService de client goi wallet qua socket.
- Cap nhat ClientMain de khoi tao WalletApi va WalletController.
- Cap nhat AuthFrame de truyen WalletController vao bidder dashboard va mo top-up dialog.
- Them TopUpDialogController va top-up-dialog.fxml.
- Cap nhat BidderDashboardViewController va bidder-dashboard-view.fxml:
  + hien thi tong so du
  + hien thi so du kha dung
  + hien thi so tien dang bi giu
  + nut TOP UP de nap tien

4. Ket qua dat duoc
- He thong da co wallet hoat dong tren dung kien truc hien tai.
- Bidder co the nap tien vao tai khoan.
- He thong chi cho phep dat bid khi so du kha dung du.
- Khi bidder dang dan dau, so tien tuong ung duoc reserve.
- Khi bi outbid, so tien reserve cu duoc release.
- Khi seller xac nhan thanh toan, he thong capture reserved funds.
- Khi ket qua dau gia bi huy, he thong release hoac refund tien tuy theo trang thai truoc do.

5. Kiem tra da thuc hien
- Da kiem tra compile bang javac cho core-common va server: thanh cong.
- Da kiem tra compile phan client da sua voi JavaFX jars co san tren may: thanh cong.
- Chua chay full Maven test vi moi truong hien tai khong co lenh mvn trong PATH.

6. Gioi han va luu y
- Database can duoc khoi tao/cap nhat de co 2 bang moi: wallets, topup_transactions.
- Top-up hien tai dang o che do mo phong xac nhan ngay, chua tich hop cong thanh toan that.
- Luong wallet hien tai tap trung vao bidder-side flow; neu can co dashboard rieng cho admin ve top-up history thi can bo sung them.

7. Huong phat trien tiep theo
- Chay thu nghiem end-to-end tren server va client that.
- Bo sung test JUnit cho WalletService va cac tinh huong bid/reserve/release/capture/refund.
- Neu can, mo rong giao dien de hien thi lich su top-up va lich su bien dong wallet.

8. Ket luan
- Chuc nang wallet da duoc dua vao ban hien tai ma khong pha vo kien truc client-server dang co.
- Phan tich hop khong chi dung o muc nap tien, ma da lien ket truc tiep voi luong dau gia va settlement, giup he thong gan hon voi nghiep vu thuc te.
